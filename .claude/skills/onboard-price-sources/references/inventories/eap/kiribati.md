## Structural conclusion (wave 11, 2026-09-01)

Kiribati has **zero scrapeable retail e-commerce** as of this pass. The two real South Tarawa retail chains (Punjas Kiribati, MOEL Trading) both have a web presence but neither has a functioning online catalog -- one is corporate-only, the other has a storefront pitch with dead "Shop Now" links. This matches the brief's framing exactly: population ~130,000 across 32 atolls, satellite-dependent internet, e-commerce close to nonexistent. The country's only realistic near-term food-price lever is **not a new website** but the Ministry of Commerce's Price (Regulation) Order archive (see row above) -- and even that is `channel: null` (official/administered price, not a live storefront) so it would add real coverage without ever moving the food-channel count. A genuine food-channel source for Kiribati would require either (a) MOEL fixing its Wix ordering flow, (b) a new storefront launching, or (c) a materially different definition of "retail" than "live web catalog with per-product pages." None of those exist today.
