## Phase 0.5 depth audit (2026-08-11)

Ask: MAFF retail/wholesale, Toyosu wholesale, and e-Stat/Statistics Bureau retail price survey were proposed as fetcher targets to fill fresh fish/seafood, produce, and specialty-food leaves. Both MAFF-family targets are **already onboarded and live**: `opengov_retail_prices` (MIC/Stat Bureau reproduction, 228 food+alcohol item codes, 15,754 rows on disk) and `opengov_wholesale_produce` (MAFF wholesale fruit/vegetable, 71 commodity codes, 1,186 rows on disk). The Toyosu fish-specific sibling page exists on opengov.jp but currently has 0 populated items (see row above) — not scaffoldable today.

The real division-01 gap is fresh/prepared seafood, edible seaweed, and tubers (COICOP leaves under 01.1.3.1–01.1.3.7, 01.1.7.4.6, 01.1.7.5.4/5). Checked against `data/prices/enrich/products_input.parquet` (raw, pre-classification) — these items are **abundant in the already-scraped corpus**, overwhelmingly at `rakuten` (634k raw JP rows): tuna/maguro 1,667 rows, eel/unagi 2,132, seaweed/kombu-wakame-nori 2,916, shrimp/ebi 1,409, dried/salted fish 392, yamaimo/taro 150, cooking oils 59. But `data/prices/build/eap_fnb_observations.parquet` (post-classification, trusted) shows almost none of these leaves populated for Japan — e.g. `01.1.3.1.5` (tuna), `01.1.3.4.4` (cephalopods fresh), `01.1.3.2.1` (smoked salmon), `01.1.3.4.1` (shrimp fresh), `01.1.3.6.x` (prepared/preserved seafood) are all absent from Japan's 120/269-leaf covered set despite the raw product names being on file. `amicashop_jp` — the source purpose-built in a prior round specifically to target seaweed/dried-fish/tuber keywords — only produced 12 trusted rows against an 88-row raw collect.

**Verdict: classifier/gold gap, not a sourcing gap.** Per the skill's Phase 0.5 outcome table, the fix is seeding gold for these leaves and retraining the classifier head, not onboarding another retailer. Do not send this ask back to `onboard-price-sources`; route to gold-growth. No new source was scaffolded this run.
