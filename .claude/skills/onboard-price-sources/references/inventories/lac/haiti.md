# Haiti

_Inventory written: 2026-09-01_

Wave 12 pass. Cold start — no `lac/caribbean/haiti/` config directory existed before
this pass; measured coverage going in was 0 sources / 0 food, the largest closeable gap
in the wave. Target: >=5 sources AND >=2 food-and-beverage sources (channel in
supermarket/hypermarket/convenience/fresh-market/specialty-food).

**Result: 5 sources / 1 food.** The 5-source bar is cleared. The 2-food bar is NOT
cleared — this is an honest shortfall, not a padded count. Every plausible food/grocery
lead surfaced by the brief's candidate list, the workbook, and six follow-up searches
was probed; all but one (Cassandra Online Market) were dead or unbuildable. See the
table below for the full trail, including dead ends, so the next pass doesn't repeat
this work.

Currency trap confirmed real for this country: sites mix HTG, "Haitian dollars" (1 HD =
5 HTG) and USD. No site probed this pass actually displayed Haitian-dollar pricing, but
two of the five shipped sources (Cassandra, Access Haiti) are genuinely USD-priced
(diaspora-facing grocery pickup and a Haiti ISP respectively) — stated explicitly in
each YAML's `notes:`, not silently treated as HTG.

## COICOP / channel gap after this pass

Haiti ends at **5 sources / 1 food**. The 5-source bar is cleared (fetchers: `wfp_prices`,
`ihsi_cpi`; spiders: `cassandraonlinemarket_ht`, `digicel_ht`, `accesshaiti_ht`). The
2-food bar is not cleared — `cassandraonlinemarket_ht` (hypermarket) is the only channel
in {supermarket, hypermarket, convenience, fresh-market, specialty-food}.

Both candidates the brief flagged as most likely to "make the food requirement
essentially done" — Caribbean Supermarket SA and Delimart Haiti, the two biggest
Port-au-Prince chains — are dead for scraping purposes (brochure-only, and
image-only-flyers respectively). The most promising remaining lead for a second food
source is **Caribbean Online Grocery** (Cluny-market vendors, Cap-Haïtien fresh produce),
currently showing a "Coming Soon" placeholder — worth a fast re-check in a future pass
since the described shape is exactly what this gap needs, and the domain is already
identified. **Makèt Pam** is the other unresolved lead (legacy JS portal, needs a
Playwright probe rather than a static fetch to determine if it's live commerce or another
demo/seed shell).

COICOP coverage after this pass: division 01 (food) has price-level coverage only via
`cassandraonlinemarket_ht` (retailer_sku, classifier-routed) and `wfp_prices` (official_avg,
markets/staples); IHSI's `ihsi_cpi` gives index-level coverage across all 12 divisions.
Divisions 04 (housing/utilities — no EDH tariff found), 07.2 (fuel — no BRH table found),
and most of 02/03/05/06/09/10/12 have no price-level source at all — genuine sourcing
gaps, not depth gaps, since Haiti had zero prior coverage of any kind.
